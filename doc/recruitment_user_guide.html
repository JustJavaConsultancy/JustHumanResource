Recruitment User Guide

This guide explains how to use the Recruitment module in JustHumanResource, from staff requisition approval through job publishing, candidate applications, interviews, offers, and onboarding handoff.

Audience: HR, Recruiters, Hiring Managers, Finance, Candidates Module: Recruitment Updated: August 24, 2026

1. What the Recruitment Module Does

The Recruitment module manages the hiring pipeline after an approved staff requisition. It creates job openings, publishes roles to the public careers page, records applications, guides HR through recruitment workflow stages, manages interviews and offers, and converts accepted candidates into employee onboarding.

Staff requisition approved Job opening created Content prepared Hiring manager review Published to careers Candidate applies Screening to offer Offer accepted Onboarding starts

4. Staff Requisition to Job Opening

  1. An employee or authorized user submits a staff requisition from the Requests module.
  2. The request follows the configured approval workflow.
  3. When the staff requisition is approved, the system automatically creates a recruitment job opening.
  4. The new opening appears under /recruitment/job-openings with draft status.
Important: Users do not manually create job openings in Recruitment. Openings are created from approved staff requisitions so recruitment remains tied to workforce planning and approvals.

5. HR: Prepare a Job Opening

  1. Open /recruitment/job-openings.
  2. Select the draft opening.
  3. Enter the publication content:
    • Job description
    • Responsibilities
    • Requirements
    • Preferred qualifications
    • Location and work arrangement
    • Application open and close dates
  4. Click Save and send for review.

The opening moves to content review and waits for the hiring manager review task.

6. Hiring Manager: Review Publication

  1. Open the job opening review task or go to the job opening detail page.
  2. Review the role title, position count, publication content, and dates.
  3. Choose one action:
    • Approve & publish makes the job visible on /careers.
    • Return sends the content back to HR for changes.
    • Cancel stops the opening workflow.

7. Public Candidate: Apply for a Job

  1. Go to /careers.
  2. Select a published job.
  3. Complete the application form with name, email, phone, profile links, and source.
  4. Tick the consent checkbox.
  5. Submit the application.
After submission, the system displays an application reference and a tracking link. Candidates should keep the tracking link because it is used to view application status and respond to offers.

8. HR: Manage the Candidate Pipeline

Open /recruitment/applications and select an application. The application detail page shows the candidate, role, current stage, workflow action, interviews, offers, onboarding action, and activity history.

9. Schedule Interviews

  1. Open the application detail page.
  2. Use the Schedule interview form.
  3. Enter title, start time, end time, location or meeting link.
  4. Enter the coordinator employee ID and panel employee IDs.
  5. Click Schedule.

The application stage is moved to Interview when an interview is scheduled.

10. Submit Interview Scorecards

  1. Open the application detail page.
  2. Find the scheduled interview under Interviews.
  3. Enter reviewer employee ID, score from 1 to 5, recommendation, and comments.
  4. Submit the scorecard.
Only assigned panel members should submit scorecards. When all assigned panel members have submitted, the interview is marked completed.

11. Create, Approve, Send, and Respond to Offers

HR: Create an Offer

  1. Open the candidate application.
  2. Use the Create offer form.
  3. Enter annual gross compensation, currency, job step, pay group, proposed start date, expiry date, and terms.
  4. Submit the offer. The offer enters approval.

Approver: Approve or Reject

  1. Open the application or offer approval task.
  2. Review compensation, dates, pay group, job step, and terms.
  3. Approve the offer or reject it.

HR: Send Approved Offer

  1. After approval, open the application detail page.
  2. Click Send on the approved offer.
  3. The offer becomes available to the candidate from the public application tracking page.

Candidate: Accept or Decline

  1. Open the application tracking link.
  2. Review the sent offer.
  3. Click Accept offer or Decline offer.
Only the latest sent offer can be answered. Expired offers cannot be accepted.

12. Start Onboarding

  1. Confirm the candidate accepted the latest offer.
  2. Open the application detail page.
  3. Complete onboarding fields such as department, manager, job step, pay group, groups, and identity details.
  4. Click Start onboarding.

The system creates an employee onboarding record and updates the application to hired/onboarding status.

13. Close or Fill Openings

  1. Open the job opening detail page.
  2. Use Close opening to stop a published or paused opening.
  3. When candidates are hired, the filled position count increases.
  4. When filled positions reach the number of approved positions, the opening is marked filled.

Closing or filling an opening completes the job-opening workflow.

15. Good Operating Practices

  • Do not publish incomplete job descriptions.
  • Set realistic application closing dates before publishing.
  • Keep interview panel employee IDs accurate.
  • Create a new offer version instead of editing a sent offer outside the system.
  • Start onboarding only after the offer is accepted.
  • Close openings that should no longer receive applications.

16. Troubleshooting

IssueWhat to Check
No job opening appears after requisition approval Confirm the request type is Staff Requisition and the request reached Approved status.
Job not visible on careers page Confirm the opening status is Published and the application close date has not passed.
Cannot submit application Confirm all required fields are completed and candidate consent is checked.
Offer cannot be sent Confirm the offer status is Approved.
Candidate cannot accept offer Confirm the offer is the latest version, status is Sent, and expiry date has not passed.
Cannot start onboarding Confirm an accepted offer exists and required onboarding fields are supplied.

17. End-to-End Checklist

  1. Staff requisition approved.
  2. Job opening automatically created.
  3. HR prepares publication content.
  4. Hiring manager approves publication.
  5. Job appears on /careers.
  6. Candidate submits application and receives reference/tracking link.
  7. HR advances candidate through screening, assessment, interview, checks, and offer stages.
  8. Interview panel submits scorecards where required.
  9. HR creates offer.
  10. Approver approves offer.
  11. HR sends offer.
  12. Candidate accepts offer from tracking page.
  13. HR starts onboarding.
  14. Opening is closed or automatically filled when approved positions are exhausted.
