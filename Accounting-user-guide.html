JustHumanResource Accounting User Guide

A practical guide for finance users who configure payroll accounting defaults, review generated journal postings, and export payroll journals for entry into the organization's accounting system.

Audience: Finance Officer / Payroll Accountant Module: Finance > Accounting Format: HTML / Print-ready

1. What The Accounting Module Does

The accounting part of JustHumanResource converts approved payroll results into balanced payroll journal entries. It uses configured ledger accounts to classify earnings, deductions, taxes, pension liabilities, employer pension costs, and net salary payable.

Configure accounts Enter default ledger codes and names in Payroll Accounting Settings.
Run payroll Payroll creates employee pay runs and payroll line items.
Post payroll The final payroll runs must reach posted status for journal generation.
Generate journal Period close generates debit and credit lines for the payroll period.
Export CSV Finance exports the journal from Financial Posting for accounting import.

2. Accessing The Finance Area

  1. Sign in to JustHumanResource with a user account that has finance access.
  2. Open the Finance area.
  3. Use the finance sidebar to open Dashboard, Approve Lock Approvals, Closed Periods, Bank Details, and Financial Posting.
  4. Open payroll accounting setup directly at /finance/accounting-settings when you need to configure ledger defaults.
The current sidebar includes Financial Posting but does not expose Accounting Settings as a main menu item. Use the direct URL or the Journal postings link from the settings page after it is opened.

3. Required Before You Start

  • Confirm the payroll period has been calculated and reviewed.
  • Confirm payroll runs for the period are posted.
  • Confirm default ledger accounts are correct for the company.
  • Confirm allowance, deduction, and pension account mappings where specific mappings are required.
  • Confirm the period is ready for finance review before exporting journals.

4. Configure Payroll Accounting Settings

Accounting settings provide fallback ledger accounts used when individual pay items do not have their own account mapping. To configure them, open /finance/accounting-settings.

  1. 1Review each account section.
  2. 2Enter the correct account code from your chart of accounts.
  3. 3Enter the matching account name.
  4. 4Click Save settings.
  5. 5Return to Financial Posting to review generated journals.
Setting Purpose Default Example
Payroll Suspense Credit account for net salary payable through payroll suspense. 2000 - Payroll Suspense
Salary Payable Salary payable ledger reference available for payroll accounting setup. 2000 - Salary Payable
Default Earnings Expense Debit account used for earnings when the allowance has no specific expense account. 5000 - Salary Expense
Default Deduction Payable Credit account used for deductions without a specific payable account. 2100 - Payroll Deductions Payable
PAYE Payable Credit account used for employee tax deductions with component code PAYE. 2110 - PAYE Tax Payable
Employee Pension Payable Credit account used for employee pension deductions when pension scheme mapping is not provided. 2120 - Pension Payable
Employer Pension Expense Debit account used for employer pension cost when scheme expense mapping is not provided. 5010 - Employer Pension Expense
Employer Pension Payable Credit account used for employer pension liability when scheme payable mapping is not provided. 2120 - Pension Payable
Use real account codes from your accounting system before closing payroll periods. Placeholder defaults are useful for setup, but they may not match the organization's chart of accounts.

5. How Journal Lines Are Created

  • Earnings create debit lines to an allowance expense account or the default earnings expense account.
  • Deductions create credit lines to PAYE, pension, deduction-specific payable accounts, or the default deduction payable account.
  • Employer pension cost creates a debit to employer pension expense and a credit to employer pension payable.
  • Net salary creates a credit to payroll suspense.
  • Out-of-payroll items and zero or negative line items are ignored by journal generation.

7. Review Financial Posting

Open Finance > Financial Posting or go to /finance/posting. This page shows unexported payroll journal entries grouped by payroll period.

  1. Check the summary cards for total periods, total entries, total debits, and balanced periods.
  2. Open each period section and review the account code, account name, description, debit amount, and credit amount.
  3. Confirm each period shows as balanced before exporting.
  4. Investigate any period marked unbalanced before using the file in an accounting system.
A healthy payroll journal has total debits equal to total credits for the period.

8. Export Payroll Journal CSV

  1. Open Financial Posting.
  2. Locate the payroll period to export.
  3. Click Export CSV.
  4. Save or upload the downloaded CSV according to your accounting import process.
  5. After export, the journal batch and entries are marked exported in the system.

Exported filenames follow the pattern payroll-journal-period-[periodId].csv.

10. Working With Period Close And Lock Approval

Journal generation is part of the payroll period close workflow. Finance users may also review lock approval tasks from Approve Lock Approvals.

  • Use Approve Lock Approvals to approve or reject pending period close requests assigned to finance.
  • Use Closed Periods to view finalized payroll periods after completion.
  • Use Financial Posting after the period close workflow has generated the journal entries.

11. No Journal Entries Showing

  • Confirm payroll runs exist for the period.
  • Confirm payroll runs are posted.
  • Confirm the period close workflow has reached journal generation.
  • Confirm entries were not already exported.

12. Missing Account Mapping

  • Open Accounting Settings and complete every required account code and name.
  • Review allowance expense accounts.
  • Review deduction payable accounts.
  • Review pension scheme payable and expense accounts.

13. Unbalanced Journal

  • Check payroll line items for the period.
  • Review employer pension configuration.
  • Confirm net pay was calculated correctly.
  • Do not import unbalanced journals into accounting.

14. Recommended Operating Controls

Control Recommended Practice
Settings change control Restrict accounting settings updates to authorized finance administrators.
Pre-export review Confirm every period is balanced and account names match expected ledger accounts.
Export retention Keep exported CSV files with payroll period records and approval evidence.
Duplicate prevention Use the exported status to avoid importing the same payroll journal twice.
Exception handling Resolve missing mappings or unbalanced journals before approving period close.
